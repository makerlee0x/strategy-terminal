SEC Innovation Exemption for Tokenized Securities Venues

The U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission approved a five-year Innovation Exemption allowing qualifying Tokenized Securities Venues (TSVs) to trade tokenized U.S. stocks onchain without registering as traditional exchanges. Temporary relief lasts five years. Tokens must represent real share ownership with voting and dividend rights. Issuer veto requires 30-day notice. Dealer exemption covers liquidity providers on approved permissioned AMMs. Market benefits include 24/7 trading, fractional shares, near-instant settlement, and investor self-custody.
